# Visual QA — Initial Directions

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Status:** Pass after one correction round  
**Target:** The chooser and four one-page concepts in `design-directions/`

## Browser evidence

Each option was rendered in the local browser at:

- 1440 × 1000 desktop
- 768 × 1024 tablet
- 390 × 844 phone

Evidence is stored in `design-directions/previews/`. Because the in-app browser's stitched `fullPage` export duplicated some scroll bands, only normal viewport captures named `desktop-top`, `desktop-mid`, `tablet`, and `mobile` are treated as visual evidence. Live DOM metrics and viewport captures did not duplicate content.

## Pass A findings

- All four pages loaded with one `h1`, no broken local images, and no horizontal document overflow at any target width.
- The directions remained visually distinct while sharing the editorial-gallery and commercial-clarity thesis.
- Direction 02's tablet wordmark overlapped its explanatory copy.
- The mobile menu buttons in Directions 01, 02, and 04 were visually present but were missing the JavaScript hook attribute.

## Corrections

- Repositioned and resized the Direction 02 tablet wordmark, then measured the title and copy rectangles: overlap is `0px` with a visible gap.
- Added `data-menu-toggle` to all three responsive menu buttons.

## Pass B verification

- All three mobile menus set `aria-expanded="true"`, expose the primary navigation, and move focus to its first link.
- Escape closes each menu and restores `aria-expanded="false"`.
- Direction 03's Cool filter sets `aria-pressed="true"`, leaves exactly the two cool sample records visible, and announces `Showing Cool sample pieces.` through its live status region.
- No browser console errors were recorded during the interaction checks.
- The corrected Direction 02 tablet hero has no title/copy overlap.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes transition and animation duration; meaningful content is present in semantic HTML before JavaScript.
- Local concept imagery was converted from 11 MB of JPEGs to 7 MB of WebP assets (about 36% smaller), then all 12 breakpoint views were reloaded with zero broken images or horizontal overflow.

## Intentionally deferred

These are design concepts, not the production shop. Real catalog data, product detail, cart, checkout, policies, localization, SEO contracts, and their loading/error/unavailable states belong in the first approved feature specification and plan.
