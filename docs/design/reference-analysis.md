# ÉPOCA Reference Analysis

## Why these references matter

The supplied screenshots and external design systems agree on one useful idea: premium commerce is strongest when art direction and product truth reinforce each other. ÉPOCA should borrow that principle without copying a competitor's page.

## Screenshot observations

### Lulu and Georgia — split hero

- A promotional utility strip and broad wordmark establish retail confidence before the editorial moment.
- The hero pairs a warm room photograph with a solid oxblood story panel.
- Large serif copy supplies character; navigation and utility copy stay restrained and functional.
- The tension between lifestyle photography and a graphic color field makes the layout memorable.

### Selency — cinematic home hero

- Search and taxonomy remain visible above a full-bleed interior image.
- Oversized serif copy is positioned as part of the photograph rather than in a detached card.
- The image carries most of the emotion; interface chrome stays quiet.

### Selency — product index

- A pale ground, editorial introduction, and disciplined four-column grid make many products feel curated rather than crowded.
- Product information is plain and useful: image, name, and price receive a stable hierarchy.
- Whitespace and consistent crops create perceived quality without decorative card styling.

### Lulu and Georgia — room stories

- Wide room vignettes behave like chapters in a magazine.
- Serif overlays, direct dark calls to action, and a narrow promotional strip keep storytelling connected to shopping.
- Repeated large image fields create rhythm without relying on a generic component grid.

## External workflow principles adopted

From Impeccable:

- Establish product and design context before generating UI.
- Shape a concrete direction, then critique, audit, harden, and polish it.
- Choose a clear page mode instead of mixing incompatible interaction vocabularies.
- Treat accessibility, performance, theming, responsive behavior, and generic-design anti-patterns as one quality review.

From Meng To's `build-awwwards-quality-sites` skill:

- Build original, reference-inspired art direction rather than copying a page.
- Use a coherent hero and complete semantic page, not an isolated visual trick.
- Use real, attributable imagery and honest content.
- Add motion only when it serves the experience, with reduced-motion and static fallbacks.
- Avoid fake testimonials, logos, award claims, generic bento grids, and gratuitous WebGL or smooth scrolling.

## ÉPOCA synthesis

The shared direction is an **editorial gallery with commercial clarity**. The four concepts deliberately test different balances:

| Direction         | Emotional lead          | Commerce lead               | Primary risk to audit              |
| ----------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Atelier Editorial | Warm fashion editorial  | Curated story links         | Copy becoming too dominant         |
| Gallery Monolith  | Architectural immersion | Minimal anchored actions    | Controls disappearing into imagery |
| Collector's Index | Connoisseurship         | Catalog grid and metadata   | Feeling too austere                |
| Living Archive    | Material history        | Curated collection chapters | Layering becoming visually noisy   |

## What ÉPOCA should not inherit

- Competitor logos, exact compositions, copy, product names, or distinctive proprietary motifs.
- Interface minimalism that hides search, price, availability, policies, or checkout feedback.
- Editorial type so extreme that product facts become difficult to scan.
- Motion that delays content, causes layout shifts, or makes browsing less predictable.
